Test 1 — is your ground contact honest?

Thirty seconds, and it could invalidate everything from yesterday. Your meter cannot tell a weak source from a bad ground — both look identical. This separates them.

Steps

  1. Build the rig above on J11-1, backlight asleep, load resistor in.
  2. Read the voltage with the black probe on the − rail. Record it as A.
  3. Move the black probe only to the USB-C shell. Dial does not change. Red probe does not move.
  4. Read again. Record as B.

Each state is judged on its own — you only need one good cell for harvesting to be worth building.

How to read the outcome
  • Wired · asleep is strong → the keyboard only powers down its LED driver on battery. Harvesting works whenever the cable is in — enough to keep the cell topped up for free, with no keyboard modification.
  • Both backlight-on rows strong → harvest while you type, coast the rest of the time. The backlight is on exactly when you are at the keyboard, so this fits well — provided no dimming is visible.
  • All four weak → harvesting gives a trickle, not a supply. The 77 mAh cell still carries the module about 66 days on its own; you would just remove it occasionally to charge, or reconsider the internal battery tap.
