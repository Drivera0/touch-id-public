TouchID Module — caliper measurement sheet rev 2 · enclosure

Reference part: the knob module (housing + PCB) and the slot. Green rows are already caliper-confirmed — re-check only if something looks off. Fill the rest, press Generate parameters, and paste the block over the parameter section of cad/scripts/touchid_module.py.

Two open questions this sheet must settle:
1 · Is the module square? The CAD lip is 19.72 × 19.72, but the PCB caliper pass gave 19.60 × 17.60 — not square. If the PCB fills the lip footprint, both can't be right. See rows 1a / 1b.
2 · Screw-head clearance. The PCB is the module's bottom face and its pogo pads press on the keyboard's spring pins. The M2 screws go up into the housing, so their heads land on that same outside face. This PCB batch was ordered with "Countersink Hole: No", so v1 has plain Ø2.2 holes — nothing recesses the heads. Row 27 decides the fix.

Sensor & retaining ledge — blocked until a sensor is chosen

The sensor drops in from inside and rests on a ledge, so finger pressure lands on the housing, not the cable or solder joints. Body Ø must be > window (12.4) and ≤ seat (14.2). R502-B (~22) and ZW101 (~21) don't fit.
